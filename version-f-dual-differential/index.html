How it works

Flywheel = (A + B) ÷ 2 · diff 1, both suns by gear
Climber = (B − A) ÷ 2 · diff 2, B's sun by chain
so A = fly − climb, B = fly + climb

A gear mesh reverses direction and a chain doesn't. Differential 2 gets B through a chain, so it sees B with the opposite sign to differential 1. That one sign is the whole trick: when A and B match, differential 2's planets spin in place and its carrier stays still. When they are opposite, the same happens in differential 1.

Build notes
  1. Make the carriers. Clamp two 125T gears together and drill them as a pair so the holes line up. Open the centre hole to the bearing's outside diameter and press in a Through Bore Bearing, so the carrier spins on the half-shaft. Drill 6 bearing holes on a 56.25 mm circle: 3 pairs 120° apart, with the 2 holes of a pair 22.5 mm apart (47.2°).
  2. Planets. Each planet shaft carries two 30T gears. Planet 1 sits in the sun-1 lane and the middle lane; planet 2 sits in the middle lane and the sun-2 lane. Planet 1 meshes sun 1 and planet 2, and planet 2 meshes sun 2. Put a collar outside each carrier plate.
  3. Stack it front to back: half-shaft (72T + sun), carrier plate, planets, carrier plate, half-shaft (sun + 72T or sprocket). All 3 pairs drop in, because 120° steps land on whole sun teeth.
  4. Keep A and B bare between the carriers. The carriers clear those shafts by only 3.5 mm, so a collar there would rub.
  5. Climb stage is doubled. Two 30T on C drive two 125T on C2 side by side (30 mm of tooth face), because plastic teeth at climb torque need the extra width.
  6. Hang lock is fail-safe. A rubber band pulls the pawl into C2's 125T and the servo holds it out. When the robot is disabled at the end of the match, the servo goes limp and the pawl drops in. File the tip as a ratchet so the climber can still wind up with the pawl in.
  7. Use velocity control. Both groups must hold exact speeds or the "still" output creeps. HD Hex encoders give 28 counts per motor turn, so 2,800 counts/s at free speed:
// fly, climb in -1..1 (|fly| + |climb| <= 1)
double a = fly - climb, b = fly + climb;
double s = Math.max(1, Math.max(Math.abs(a), Math.abs(b)));
groupA1.setVelocity(a / s * 2800); groupA2.setVelocity(a / s * 2800);
groupB1.setVelocity(b / s * 2800); groupB2.setVelocity(b / s * 2800);

View

Compared with E: twice the motors, no shift delay, both outputs can run at once, and the servo moves to a hang lock instead of a shifter. The cost is more gears (49 against 9), two drilled carriers per differential, and the need for encoder velocity control. This is a layout model in millimetres, not a CAD file.
