What's inside (all of it)
  • Camshaft shifter. An engine-style camshaft, turned by a servo with an absolute encoder, has 6 cams that push the forks, brakes and the park pawl through 7 positions.
  • Synchronizer cones on all 3 dog clutches match speeds before the dogs lock, so the motors never stop to shift.
  • 2-speed planetary flywheel. Sun 36T, planets 12T, ring 60T. Band brake on the ring gives a 2.67:1 spin-up gear; a direct clutch gives 1:1 cruise.
  • Boost. Keeps the spinning flywheel connected while the climber hauls, dumping its 326 J into the lift.
  • Cycloidal 8:1 final drive (8 lobes, 9 pins) on the haul output, coaxial with shaft C.
  • Open differential on the climb output: two spools, so both hooks pull with equal force.
  • Park pawl on shaft B locks the whole gear row.
  • Slip clutch on the flywheel pulley protects the belt during Boost.
  • Still has everything from C: the inline-4 motor row, belt drive and bearing bridges.

View

Selector positions sit 51.4° apart on the camshaft, so going from P to B rolls through every gear in order, like a motorcycle. The synchronizers make that safe at full motor speed. Layout model in inches, not a CAD file.
